About the System

The Nyeri poly Student Accommodation Portal System is designed to simplify hostel allocation and management. Students can apply online, Hostel Managers manage rooms, while Administrators oversee the entire system from one dashboard.

System Features

Students

Register accounts, apply for hostel rooms, track applications and payments online.

Hostel Managers

Manage rooms, approve applications, and monitor occupancy.

Administrator

Manage users, generate reports, and control the entire system.

Notifications

Receive instant updates for room allocations, payments and announcements.
